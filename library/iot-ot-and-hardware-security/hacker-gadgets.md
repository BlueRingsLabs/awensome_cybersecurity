# Hacker Gadgets

Sooner or later if you are IT guy, interested in security and hacking, you will start reading about various technologies, devices and electronics used by ethical hackers or penetration testers. I call it “toys for big boys”. I have a few of them and plan to buy more, as you can easily guess, my wife is not very happy about it. ## TP-Link TL-WR802N

![TL-WR802N-04](TL-WR802N-04.jpg)

[300Mbps Wireless N Nano Router](https://www.tp-link.com/en/home-networking/wifi-router/tl-wr802n/). Pocket-size ideal for travel use. I always have it with me on my holiday or business trip. ## USB killer

![USBKill-V4](USBKill-V4.jpg)

The [USBKill](https://usbkill.com/products/usbkill-v4?variant=32836117397586) is a device that stress tests hardware. When plugged in, power is taken from a USB-Port, multiplied, and discharged into the data-lines, typically disabling an unprotected device. The new models have the option of remote activation from the phone or after a specified time.
