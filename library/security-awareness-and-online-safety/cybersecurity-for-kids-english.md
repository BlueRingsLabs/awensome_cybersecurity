Vírus/Malwares
• Mal-wa-res is a short word for "malicious
software," which means computer
programs that are made to cause harm or
do bad things to computers and other
devices, such as cell phones. It's like a virus
that can make a computer sick. People who
create malware may try to steal
information, such as your game passwords
or personal data about you such as your
name, home address and the like, or even
cause your computer to stop working
properly.
Vírus/Malwares -
Example
•
Imagine you are playing Minecraft or Roblox, which are
games where you can build things and explore different
worlds. Malware would be like someone who enters your
game without you knowing and starts destroying
everything you've built or steals your precious items. This
"someone" is not a real person, but a computer program
that someone created to cause problems.
•
For example, if you download a Minecraft mod from an
untrustworthy website, that mod may have malware.
Instead of just adding cool things to your game, it can also
allow someone to control your computer or steal your
information. It's as if, while you're focused on playing,
someone invisible is going through your things without
you knowing.
Antivírus
•
Antivirus is like a superhero for your computer or cell
phone. It helps protect your device against "bad guys"
called viruses or malware that try to damage your device
or steal important information.
•
You can think of antivirus as a guard that keeps an eye on
everything that enters your computer, such as programs,
games or files you download from the internet. If it sees
something suspicious or dangerous, like a virus trying to
get in, it acts quickly to block that virus and prevent it
from causing any problems. The antivirus can also scan all
files that are already on your computer to make sure
nothing dangerous is hiding there.
•
It's like you have a very attentive friend who looks around
to make sure everything is safe while you play or do your
work on the computer. Thanks to antivirus, you can use
your device with greater peace of mind, knowing that
someone is helping to protect you and your things.
Email Security
• Let's talk about how to keep your email
safe, especially from something called
"phishing." Phishing is a trick used by
malicious people to try to trick you and
steal important information, such as
passwords or your family details.
• Imagine you receive an email that appears
to be from your favorite online game,
telling you that you've won a super cool
reward. But to get it, you need to click on a
link and enter your password. Sounds fun,
right? However, if that email isn't actually
from the game but from a phishing scam,
clicking on it could lead to bad people
knowing your password.
Email Security
To stay safe, here are some tips:
• Check who sent the email: Take a close look at
the sender's email address. If it seems strange
or different from normal, it could be a warning
sign.
• Don't click on suspicious links: If you are not
sure about a link, better not to click. Instead, go
directly to the website by typing the address
into your browser bar.
• Ask an Adult: If you get something unexpected
and you're not sure, it's a good idea to show it
to an adult before you do anything.
Password Security
A password is like a secret key that only you know and use to log
into your online accounts, such as email, games or social
networks. It is very important that this key is difficult for others to
guess so that no one can access your personal information.
To have a strong password, think of a combination that includes:
• Uppercase and lowercase letters: Mix large and small letters.
• Numbers: Add some numbers to make the password more
difficult.
• Symbols: Use symbols like @, #, $, %, which are like special
designs on the keyboard.
• Length: A long password is generally more secure, so try to
make your password at least 8 characters long.
• Don't use things that are easy to guess, like your name,
birthday, or very simple words. And remember, it's not good to
use the same password for everything; If someone discovers a
password, they won't be able to access all of your accounts.
• Thinking about a password can be like creating a secret code
that only you know how to decipher!
Safe Internet
Browsing
•
Browsing the internet can be like exploring a
huge amusement park: there are so many
amazing places to visit and interesting things to
learn. But just like at a park, it's important to
follow some safety rules to ensure you have a fun
and safe experience.
Here are some tips for safe internet browsing:
•
Know the safe ways: Only use trusted websites.
You can check whether a site is secure by looking
at its address, which should start with "https" —
the "s" at the end stands for "secure."
•
Keep your personal information safe: Just like you
wouldn't give your name, address, or phone
number to strangers on the street, don't share
this information online unless it's absolutely
necessary and the site is trustworthy.
Safe Internet
Browsing
•
Beware of strangers: On the internet, it's not always easy to
know who's on the other side of the screen. Don't accept friend
requests or respond to messages from people you don't know.
•
Use protection tools: Install a good antivirus and keep it
updated. It will help protect your computer against malicious
programs that try to invade your machine or steal your
information.
•
Think before you click: Links in emails, instant messages or on
social media may not be what they seem. Clicking on suspicious
links can take you to dangerous websites or cause you to
unintentionally download viruses.
•
Update your software: Keeping your operating system, browser,
and other software up to date is crucial to protecting your
computer from new threats, as updates often include security
fixes.
•
By following these simple tips, you can enjoy all the good things
the internet has to offer, while staying safe and secure online.
Online Gaming
Security
•
Playing online is a fun way to stay entertained and
make friends, but it's also important to play safely to
protect your information and ensure the fun
continues. Here are some online gaming safety tips:
•
Use safe nicknames: When creating your character
or profile in games, choose nicknames that do not
reveal your real identity. Avoid using your full name,
date of birth or any personal information.
•
Strong passwords: As with other online accounts,
use strong and unique passwords for your gaming
accounts. A good password contains a combination
of letters, numbers and symbols.
•
Be careful with links and downloads: Be cautious
when clicking on links or downloading files within
games, especially if they come from people you
don't know. They may contain viruses or malware.
Online Gaming
Security
•
Privacy Settings: Adjust your privacy settings in games to
control who can see your profile and communicate with you.
Limit access to information you don't want to share.
•
Secure communication: Be careful when sharing personal
information with other players. Remember that people online
may not be who they say they are.
•
Parental supervision: For younger players, it is important that
parents or guardians are involved. They can help set up
accounts, adjust privacy settings, and monitor gaming
activities.
•
Following these tips can help keep your online gaming
experience safe and enjoyable, allowing you to focus on fun
and competition in a protected environment.
