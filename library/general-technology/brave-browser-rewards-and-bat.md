# Brave browser, rewards and BAT

Few years ago I was looking for solution to block advertisements on my Android phone. There were few options, but most of them were - let’s say - not too easy to implement or not intuitive. Today we have a few Android applications which easily blocks ads not only in browser, but also in applications and games. For example [AdGuard](https://adguard.com/) or [AdLock](https://adlock.com/). But this article will not be about ad blockers, at least not entirely.

![brave](brave.jpg)

## Brave

During my research I found web browser called [Brave](https://brave.com/). It’s got ad blocker build in. Brave is available on iOS, Windows, Linux and Android. This is open source project focused on privacy. Brave is also much faster than other browsers and helps not to download unwanted content, Brave blocks ads and trackers, so you don’t pay for them (in the context of mobile traffic).

### Features

- Shields

  Ad blocking
  Fingerprinting prevention
  Cookie control
  HTTPS upgrading
  Block scripts
  Per-site shield settings
  Configurable global shield defaults
- Tabs & Windows

  Private Windows
  Pinned Tabs
  Auto-unload
  Drag and drop
  Duplicate
  Close Options
  Find on page
  Print page
- Address Bar

  Add Bookmark
  Autosuggest URLs
  Search from address bar
  Autosuggest search terms
  Show/hide bookmarks toolbar
  Show secure or insecure site
- Security

  Clear browsing data
  Built-in password manager
  Form autofill
  Control content access to full-screen presentation
  Control site access to autoplay media
  Send “Do not track” with browsing requests
- Search

  Choose default search engine
  Use keyboard shortcuts for alternate search engines
  Option to use DuckDuckGo for private window search
- Extensions/Plugins
  Brave Desktop now supports most of the Chrome extensions in the chrome web store

Almost everything you need without additional extensions.

Personally I am using [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/Home)/[Chrome](https://www.google.com/intl/en/chrome/) and sometimes [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/). I tested a few other web browsers but never found any good as these two. I started using Brave on my smart phone and it made a big impression on me. So I gave it a chance on my laptop/desktop. It’s cool. This is why I wrote this article. Give a chance for Brave, it will not disappoint you.

You can [download Brave here](https://brave.com/utr756).

## Brave Rewards

Brave also started something new on advertisement market. I completely understand this, since they contribute to the blocking of ads they want to show that everyone can support publishers in different way. Everyone who publishes on the Internet would like to make money on it, so everyone puts ads on websites. Some ads are intrusive, or there are too many of them. It’s annoying and resource-consuming (on mobile it’s also expensive).

Brave introduced their own system to contribute publishers.

> How does it work?
>
> Readers may choose a monthly contribution amount which is divided among the publisher sites they visit most. As Brave grows, so does your revenue. Here is the best part. Neither the readers nor the publisher need to do anything different. They just keep surfing and you just keep publishing.
>
> Well, one small detail… you do need to collect your cash.

Get more information here: <https://brave.com/creators/>

Here you can register: <https://creators.brave.com/>

## Basic Attention Token

All payments in Brave Rewards are organized by default, using [Basic Attention Token](https://basicattentiontoken.org/) (BAT) *Blockchain-based digital advertising* and you can also switch for other popular crypto-currencies and standard currencies (you have to add currency exchange fees to all transaction then).

If you want to get some free BATs, [download and install Brave](https://brave.com/utr756) browser. Run it and hit triangle icon next to URL bar. Claim 20 free BAT’s (depends on current exchange rate it’s about 10 USD at the moment). You need to use Brave for a few days. From time to time you will get a chance to grab another free part of BAT’s. Collect them and share with your favorite publishers.

The whole project looks promising. I’m not sure if the project will gain popularity, but I keep my fingers crossed. The idea is cool, the implementation is good, the hardest will be to convince users to this solution.

The easiest way is to overwhelm people with commercials and earn money. It does not require action on their part.

As you do not have to buy anything or invest anything, take a moment and grab these free BATs and give a tip your favorite websites.
