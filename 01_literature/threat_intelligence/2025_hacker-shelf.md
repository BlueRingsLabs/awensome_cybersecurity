# Hacker Shelf

Hello, readers of my blog. Today, I would like to present my new baby to you. More specifically, a project that is simply an online library containing PDF files on the subject of cybersecurity. Specifically, it covers pentesting, hacking, tools, red teaming, blue team, forensics, AI, risk, networks, malware analysis, threat hunting, and so on. Everything is more or less related to security.

![HackerShelf](hackershelf.webp)

Did anyone ask for such a website? No. Does anyone even need it? Probably not. Did I do it to make money? No. So why the hell did I do it? I don’t know.

Nah, I know. If you are regular readers, you are familiar with the ‘fill the gap’ series entitled ‘Worth checking.’ When I don’t have time or inspiration, I publish this article to have at least one per month, and it contains links to interesting articles, websites, graphics, and other stuff, including PDF files.

So I thought it would be cool to present all these PDFs in one place so that everyone could easily search for and download them depending on what they need. Privately, I use the [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) application to organise my ebooks, comics and PDF files. There is also [Calibre-Web](https://github.com/janeczku/calibre-web), which allows you to publish your library in the form of a web interface. I really like these types of interfaces. Online book stores and e-book organisation applications simply look beautiful.

And then I thought, I’ll make my own hacker library with free PDF files on this topic. So I just sat down with my best friend Chat-GPT and got to work. In two days, [HackerShelf.xyz](https://hackershelf.xyz/) was created. I achieved my goal. I created a simple, transparent library of PDF files that only has a cover, title, author, tags, and a simple download counting mechanism. Additionally, there is a search engine that searches by words from the title and tags. I also divided the PDFs into categories. There is pagination because endless scrolling sucks. I also implemented an RSS feed so that newly added PDFs can be tracked. I also hid a small administrator dashboard for uploading books and managing tags and categories. The whole thing is [PHP](https://www.php.net/) and a [SQLite](https://sqlite.org/) database.

This is another [project](https://reycdxyc24gf7jrnwutzdn3smmweizedy7uojsa7ols6sflwu25ijoyd.onion/projects) for my collection, which I created because I could and wanted to learn something new and organise what I share with people in a slightly better way. I won’t hide the fact that sometimes I need to quickly find a cheatsheet or guide because I’m doing something or want to do something new, so I search my resources and get to work. This library will help me with that.

I don’t know how long the project will last or how much I will develop it. For now, it is what it was supposed to be and does what I wanted it to do, so if you like it, feel free to use it. In all subsequent ‘Worth Checking’ posts, when I add new PDF materials, I will always add them to this library as well.

If you have any interesting PDF files on related topics, please send them to me and I will be happy to add them. Let this be not only my library, but yours as well. Just remember that these can only be free materials that allow for further publication. I will not post anything illegal there.

As always, if you see any errors, have any suggestions, or just want to tell me I’m doing a good job, you know how to reach me.

Thank you for your attention, and see you next time!
