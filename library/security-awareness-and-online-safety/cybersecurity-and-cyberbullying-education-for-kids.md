CYBERSECURITY AND
CYBERBULLYING
EDUCATION FOR KIDS 1
Teach your child
to be cautious in
their browsing,
identify scams,
malicious
software. In
addition to the
use of tools to
have safer
internet browsing
Teach your child to
develop their self-
esteem, emotional
intelligence and accept
themselves as they are,
this will help them deal
with mean comments on
the internet
Raise awareness early on
about the importance of
strong and different
passwords on your social
media accounts, games, and
any other website you use.
Do not use for example:
janedoe123, but
*Sp1d3rM4N*, always mixing
uppercase, lowercase and
special characters

Encourage your child to
be moderate in relation
to internet use and to be
careful with words
spoken on the internet,
the feeling of anonymity
is completely false and
offensive words can lead
to many problems.

Instead of banning
internet use, make your
child aware of internet
risks such as predators,
information theft, sexting
and cyberbullying
