Conclusion
The OSCP certification is one of the most coveted and feared at the same time, I am on my
journey in search of it and I saw that many colleagues are lost in relation to content, so I
created this framework that I hope will be useful.
But in summary, practice a lot in the laboratories and study the material well, develop reports
also to have the habit of writing something that pleases the examiners, today it is much
easier to conquer it, because there is plenty of contenton the internet, so I did this favor to
help catalog this content.
