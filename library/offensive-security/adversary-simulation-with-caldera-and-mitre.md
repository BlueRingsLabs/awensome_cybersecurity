What is adversary simulation?
“Adversary simulation is a cybersecurity assessment method that
aims to test an organization's security controls against the tactics,
techniques, and procedures (TTPs) used by threat actors that pose
the greatest risk to its industry .”

What is Caldera?
“Caldera™é uma estrutura de segurança cibernética desenvolvida
