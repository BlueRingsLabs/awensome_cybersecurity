What is ChatGPT
• ChatGPT is a chatbot launched by OpenAI in November 2022. It is 
built on top of OpenAI's GPT-3.5 family of large language models, and
is fine-tuned with both supervised and reinforcement learning 
techniques.
• ChatGPT was launched as a prototype on November 30, 2022, and 
quickly garnered attention for its detailed responses and articulate 
answers across many domains of knowledge. Its uneven factual 
accuracy was identified as a significant drawback.
